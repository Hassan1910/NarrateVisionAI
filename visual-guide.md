# Visual Guide for Creating "The Power of Consistency" Video

## Step 1: Open the Application
Open your browser and go to http://localhost:3000

## Step 2: Enter the Narration Text
![Step 2](https://i.imgur.com/example1.jpg)
- Paste the optimized narration text into the "Narration Text" field
- Make sure to include all line breaks as shown in the implementation guide

## Step 3: Select Options
![Step 3](https://i.imgur.com/example2.jpg)
- Voice: Choose "alloy" from the dropdown
- Image Style: Select "natural" 
- Video Effect: Choose "Image Sequence"

## Step 4: Generate the Video
![Step 4](https://i.imgur.com/example3.jpg)
- Click the "Generate Video" button
- Wait for the processing to complete (this may take a few minutes)

## Step 5: Review and Download
![Step 5](https://i.imgur.com/example4.jpg)
- Once processing is complete, you'll see your video
- Use the play button to preview it
- Click the download button to save the video

## What to Expect
- The system will generate approximately 4 images based on your narration
- Each image will be shown during its corresponding segment of the narration
- The audio will play your narration with the selected voice
- Text overlays will show the current segment's text

## Troubleshooting
- If the video doesn't generate properly, try using shorter text segments
- If images don't match your expectations, try being more specific in your descriptions
- If you see a message about FFmpeg, follow the installation instructions provided
